Korean packaged food must show 19 allergen groups in an allergen box (알레르기 유발물질) (MFDS food labeling standard, checked Nov 2026). Other ingredients appear only in the ingredient list (원재료명), so check both. Freshly made or unpackaged food may have no allergen box.
