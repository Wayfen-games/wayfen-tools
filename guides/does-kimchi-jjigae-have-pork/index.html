Does Kimchi Jjigae Have Pork? Kimchi Stew, Broth and What to Ask

Open the free tool →

Very often, yes. The most common kimchi stew is cooked with pork (돼지고기), and some places add Spam or ham. Other versions use canned tuna (참치) or anchovy stock. The kimchi itself usually contains fish sauce or salted shrimp.

Sentences to show

김치찌개에 돼지고기나 햄이 들어가요?Does the kimchi stew have pork or ham?
국물은 뭐로 내요? 고기 육수예요, 멸치 육수예요?What is the broth made from? Meat stock or anchovy stock?

What to know

  • The broth can look much the same whether it is pork, anchovy or plain water. Ask about the broth, not only the meat you can see.
  • "Tuna kimchi stew" (참치김치찌개) has no pork in the name, but check the broth and the kimchi.
  • Army stew (부대찌개) almost always has ham, sausage and Spam.
  • Vegans: even without meat, restaurant kimchi usually has fish sauce or salted shrimp.
  • Halal: even without pork, meat stock is usually not halal.
