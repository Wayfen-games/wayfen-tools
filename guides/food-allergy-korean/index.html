Korean Food Allergy Phrases for Travelers

Open the free tool →

If you have a food allergy, a clear sentence in Korean is safer than pointing at a translation app. Here are the phrases, and the words to look for on Korean food labels.

Phrases to show the staff

저는 땅콩과 견과류에 심한 알레르기가 있어요.I have a severe allergy to peanuts and tree nuts.
저는 갑각류와 조개, 연체류에 심한 알레르기가 있어요.I have a severe allergy to crustaceans and mollusks.
저는 참깨에 알레르기가 있어요. 참기름도 위험해요.I am allergic to sesame. Sesame oil is also dangerous.
저는 셀리악병이 있어서 밀, 보리, 호밀을 먹으면 크게 아파요.I have celiac disease. Wheat, barley and rye make me very sick.
같은 기름이나 도구를 같이 쓰나요?Do you use the same oil or tools for other food?

Reading labels at convenience stores

Korean packaged food lists major allergens in an allergen box marked 알레르기 유발물질. Words to look for include 땅콩 (peanut), 호두 (walnut), 잣 (pine nut), 새우 (shrimp), 게 (crab), 밀 (wheat), 대두 (soy) and 우유 (milk). Sesame is not in the legally required list, so also check the ingredient list (원재료명) for 참깨 or 참기름.

A cross-contact warning looks like 같은 제조시설에서 제조 (“made in the same facility”).

To see which of 44 common dishes usually contain these allergens, see the Korean food allergen chart.

In an emergency

Call 119 for an ambulance. The free tool has an SOS card that asks staff to call 119. These phrases help you communicate. They are not medical advice, and they cannot guarantee a dish is safe. Always carry your prescribed medication.
