Is Bibimbap Vegetarian? How to Order It Without Meat in Korea

Open the free tool →

Not by default. Most restaurants top bibimbap with seasoned beef (sometimes pork) and a fried egg. The vegetables look safe, but some namul are seasoned with fish sauce or salted shrimp.

Sentences to show

비빔밥 고기 빼고 만들어 주실 수 있어요?Can you make the bibimbap without meat?
나물이나 양념에 액젓, 젓갈, 고기 육수가 들어가요?Do the namul or the sauce contain fish sauce, salted seafood or meat broth?
달걀도 빼 주세요.Please leave out the egg too.

What to check

  • Meat topping: usually beef, sometimes pork. Ask them to leave it out before it's cooked, not picked off after.
  • Egg: a fried or raw egg on top is standard. Vegans should ask to leave it out.
  • Gochujang sauce: usually contains wheat, and many restaurants use yak-gochujang (약고추장), stir-fried with minced beef. Ask, or ask for plain gochujang on the side.
  • Namul: most are seasoned with sesame oil and soy sauce, but some use fish sauce or salted shrimp.

Temple-food restaurants usually serve bibimbap with no meat, fish or egg (and no garlic or onion), but still ask. For other dishes, see how to say "I'm vegan" in Korean.
