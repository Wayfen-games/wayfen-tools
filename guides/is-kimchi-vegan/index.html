Is Kimchi Vegan? What's in It and What to Ask in Korea

Open the free tool →

Usually not. Most kimchi served in Korean restaurants is made with fish sauce (액젓) and salted shrimp (새우젓), and some versions add oysters or anchovy. It is vegetables on the outside, seafood in the seasoning.

Sentences to show

이 김치에 젓갈이나 액젓이 들어가요?Does this kimchi contain salted seafood or fish sauce?
저는 비건이라 젓갈, 액젓, 굴이 들어간 김치는 못 먹어요.I'm vegan, so I can't eat kimchi with salted seafood, fish sauce or oysters.
김치는 빼 주셔도 괜찮아요.It's OK to leave out the kimchi.

Where kimchi hides

  • Side dishes: kimchi comes free with almost every Korean meal, so ask before eating.
  • Cooked dishes: kimchi stew, kimchi fried rice, kimchi pancakes and kimchi dumplings use the same kimchi. Kimchi stew usually adds pork, tuna or anchovy broth too.
  • Other kimchi types: radish kimchi (깍두기), white kimchi (백김치) and water kimchi (물김치) are milder, but they can still use fish sauce or salted shrimp. Ask about each one.

Finding vegan kimchi

  • Temple food and vegan restaurants: they often make kimchi without seafood (and Buddhist temple kimchi also skips garlic, green onions and other pungent vegetables). Still ask.
  • In stores: look for 비건 (vegan) or 비건 김치 on the package, and check the ingredient list (원재료명) for 액젓, 젓갈, 새우젓, 멸치 and 굴.
  • Recipes differ by restaurant and brand, so a kimchi that was fine once may not be fine the next time.
