Is Korean BBQ Halal? What Muslim Travelers Should Know

Open the free tool →

Usually not. Pork belly (삼겹살) is the most popular Korean BBQ meat, grills and tongs are often shared, marinades for bulgogi and galbi often include cooking wine (맛술), and beef or chicken is rarely halal-certified.

Sentences to show

저는 무슬림이라 돼지고기와 술을 못 먹어요.I'm Muslim, so I can't eat pork or alcohol.
할랄 인증 고기가 있어요?Do you have halal-certified meat?
양념에 맛술이나 술이 들어가요? 돼지고기와 같은 불판을 써요?Does the marinade contain cooking wine or alcohol? Do you use the same grill as pork?

What to check

  • Pork: samgyeopsal, moksal and many galbi are pork. Galbi can be beef or pork, so ask.
  • Alcohol: bulgogi and galbi marinades often contain cooking wine or mirin.
  • Cross-contact: shared grills, tongs and scissors are common at BBQ restaurants.
  • Side dishes and sauces: ssamjang and kimchi usually have no pork, but store-made soybean pastes and soy sauce often contain added alcohol (ethanol), and some kimchi uses meat broth or seasoning powder. Ask if unsure.

Safer options

Look for halal-certified or Muslim-friendly restaurants, especially around Itaewon near the Seoul Central Mosque. Muslim-friendly is not the same as halal-certified, and some serve alcohol. See halal food in Korea and Korean food without pork.
