Is Korean Soy Sauce Gluten-Free? Soy Sauce, Gochujang and Doenjang for Celiacs

Open the free tool →

Usually not. Most Korean soy sauce (간장) is brewed with wheat, store-bought gochujang (고추장) often contains wheat or barley, and many factory doenjang (된장) pastes add wheat flour. Because they are seasonings, not ingredients you can see, staff may not think of them when they answer "no wheat".

Sentences to show

저는 밀(글루텐)을 먹으면 안 돼요. 간장, 고추장, 된장에도 밀이 들어 있을 수 있어요.I can't eat wheat (gluten). Soy sauce, gochujang and soybean paste can also contain wheat.
이 음식에 간장, 고추장, 된장이 들어가요?Does this dish contain soy sauce, gochujang or soybean paste?

Where they hide

  • Soy sauce: bulgogi, galbi and other marinated meat, japchae, stir-fried side dishes, dipping sauces, braised dishes (jorim).
  • Gochujang: bibimbap sauce, tteokbokki, spicy stir-fries (jeyuk), many spicy soups.
  • Doenjang and ssamjang: soybean paste stew, the dip served at Korean BBQ.
  • Also check: batter and frying mix (부침가루, 튀김가루), noodles, fish cake, and beef stock powder.

What helps

  • Plain grilled meat with salt, plain white rice, and fresh vegetables are the easiest starting points, but the grill may be shared with marinated meat, and mixed-grain rice can contain barley. Ask for sauce on the side.
  • Gluten-free soy sauce exists (look for 무글루텐 or 글루텐프리 on the label; 밀 무첨가 alone does not mean gluten-free), but restaurants rarely use it.
  • Cross-contact is common in small kitchens. If you are celiac, ask every time and carry your own snacks.
