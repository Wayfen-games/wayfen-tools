Is Tteokbokki Vegan? Fish Cake, Anchovy Broth and What to Ask

Open the free tool →

Usually not. Typical tteokbokki from street stalls and snack shops is cooked with fish cake (어묵), often in anchovy broth (멸치 육수), and sometimes with a boiled egg. The rice cakes themselves are often wheat-based (밀떡) rather than rice (쌀떡).

Sentences to show

떡볶이에 어묵, 멸치 육수, 달걀이 들어가요?Does the tteokbokki contain fish cake, anchovy broth or egg?
어묵 빼고, 멸치 육수 말고 맹물로 만들어 주실 수 있어요?Could you make it without fish cake, using plain water instead of anchovy broth?

What to know

  • Street stalls cook one big pan in advance, so asking for a special version usually isn't possible there. Sit-down tteokbokki restaurants may be able to.
  • Gochujang usually contains wheat, and the sauce may include seasoning powder with seafood or beef.
  • Rabokki (라볶이) adds instant noodles, whose soup powder often contains beef or seafood.
  • Gluten-free travelers: even rice-cake tteokbokki usually has wheat in the gochujang.
