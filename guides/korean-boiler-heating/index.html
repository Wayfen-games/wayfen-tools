How to Use a Korean Boiler: Floor Heating (Ondol), Hot Water and the Wall Panel

Open the free tool →

Most Korean homes are heated through the floor (온돌, ondol) by a gas boiler. The control panel is usually on a wall in the kitchen or living room, and the buttons are in Korean. Here is what they mean.

Words on the panel

  • 전원: power
  • 난방: heating (warms the floor)
  • 온수: hot water. On many panels this switches to hot-water-only mode and turns the floor heating off.
  • 외출: away mode, keeps a low temperature
  • 예약: timer, runs for a set time at regular intervals
  • 실내 온도 / 온수 온도: room temperature / hot water temperature

Panels differ by brand. If you are not sure, ask your host before pressing many buttons.

It's cold

  • Make sure the panel is on and set to 난방, then raise the temperature.
  • Be patient: floor heating can take 30 to 60 minutes or more to feel warm, depending on the building.
  • Keep windows closed. Heating the floor of an empty room all day can be expensive for your host; use away mode when you go out if the house rules ask for it.

No hot water

  • Let the water run for a minute or two.
  • Check that the panel is switched on.
  • Turn the faucet handle to the left for hot water.
  • If the panel shows an error number, send a photo of it to your host.

Safety

  • If you smell gas, do not touch any switches. Open the windows, go outside and call 119.
  • Do not put clothes or anything else on the boiler itself (often on the balcony or in a utility room).
