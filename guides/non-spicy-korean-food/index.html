Non-Spicy Korean Food: What to Order and What to Say

Not everything in Korea is spicy, but red chili shows up in places you might not expect. Here is what is usually mild, what can surprise you, and how to ask for less heat.

The phrase to show

맵지 않게 만들어 주실 수 있을까요? 고춧가루, 고추장, 청양고추를 빼 주세요.Could you make it not spicy? Please leave out chili powder, gochujang and Cheongyang (hot green) peppers.
안 되면 맵지 않은 메뉴를 추천해 주세요.If that's not possible, please recommend a dish that isn't spicy.
소스는 따로 주세요.Sauce on the side, please.
덜 맵게 해 주실 수 있을까요?Could you make it less spicy?

Usually not spicy

  • Bulgogi (marinated beef), galbi (grilled ribs), samgyeopsal (grilled pork belly — skip the raw green chilies and spicy scallion salad served with it)
  • Samgyetang (chicken soup with rice), seolleongtang and gomtang (beef soups) — add salt at the table
  • Japchae (glass noodles), gimbap (except spicy versions like ttaengcho-gimbap), gyeran-mari (rolled omelet), jeon (savory pancakes — except kimchi-jeon and gochu-jeon), miyeok-guk (seaweed soup)
  • Jjajangmyeon (black bean noodles), dakjuk or jeonbok-juk (rice porridge), hotteok and bungeoppang (sweet street snacks)

Can be spicy, even if it looks mild

  • Doenjang-jjigae (soybean paste stew): many restaurants add chili or green peppers.
  • Bibimbap: comes with gochujang (red chili paste). Ask for it on the side and add a little.
  • Naengmyeon: the cold broth version (mul-naengmyeon) is mild, but many places top it with a red chili paste — ask them to leave it out. Bibim-naengmyeon is spicy. The mustard can also sting.
  • Fried chicken: plain (huraideu) is mild; yangnyeom (sauced) is sweet and spicy.
  • Side dishes: kimchi and many seasoned vegetables have chili flakes.

Almost always spicy

Kimchi-jjigae, sundubu-jjigae, budae-jjigae, tteokbokki, jeyuk-bokkeum, jjamppong, ramyeon and gamjatang. The chili is the base of these dishes, so asking to leave it out rarely works. Ask for a milder version (덜 맵게) or for a recommendation instead.

Useful words

  • 안 맵게 (an maep-ge) — not spicy · 덜 맵게 (deol maep-ge) — less spicy
  • 고춧가루 chili powder · 고추장 chili paste · 청양고추 hot green pepper
