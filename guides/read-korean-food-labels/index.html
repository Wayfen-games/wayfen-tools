How to Read Allergen Labels at Korean Convenience Stores

Open the free tool →

Korean packaged food lists major allergens in a box marked 알레르기 유발물질 (allergens). Learn a few words and you can check snacks and meals at any convenience store.

Words in the allergen box

  • 땅콩 peanut · 호두 walnut · 잣 pine nut
  • 새우 shrimp · 게 crab · 오징어 squid · 조개류 shellfish (incl. oyster 굴, abalone 전복, mussel 홍합)
  • 고등어 mackerel
  • 우유 milk · 알류, 난류 or 계란 egg
  • 밀 wheat · 메밀 buckwheat · 대두 soybean
  • 돼지고기 pork · 쇠고기 beef · 닭고기 chicken
  • 복숭아 peach · 토마토 tomato · 아황산류 sulfites

Not in the required list

Sesame, almond, cashew and barley are not legally required in the allergen box. Check the ingredient list (원재료명) for 참깨 or 참기름 (sesame), 아몬드, 캐슈넛 and 보리 (barley).

Cross-contact warning

A sentence with 같은 제조시설에서 제조 means the product was made in the same facility as the allergens it names. It means traces are possible: if your allergy is severe, treat it as unsafe. No warning does not guarantee the product is free of traces.

Freshly made or unpackaged food may have no allergen box. Rules checked in November 2026 against the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety labeling standard (MFDS). The free tool below shows these words for your own diet.
