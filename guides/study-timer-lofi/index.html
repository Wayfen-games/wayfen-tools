Free Pomodoro Timer with Lofi Music (25/5, 50/10, 90/20)

The Pomodoro method splits work into focused blocks and short breaks. This timer plays calm lofi music while you focus and rings a soft chime when it is time to rest.

How to use it

  • Pick a rhythm: 25 minutes of focus and 5 of rest, 50/10, or 90/20 for deep work.
  • Press play. Music starts with the timer.
  • At each switch you hear a chime. You can keep the music on during breaks or pause it.
  • Sessions you finish today are counted at the top.

Works on your phone

Add it to your home screen and it opens like an app. After the first visit the timer works without an internet connection (songs you have played are saved too), and it asks your phone to keep the screen on while the timer runs.
