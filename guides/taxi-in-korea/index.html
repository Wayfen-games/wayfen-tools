Taking a Taxi in Korea: Show Your Address in Korean

Open the free tool →

Many taxi drivers in Korea don't read English addresses, and hotel names in English often don't match the Korean name. The easiest way is to show the address in Korean.

Before you go

  • Copy your hotel's Korean address from your booking or a map app (Naver Map or Kakao Map show it in Korean).
  • Paste it into the free tool below. It stays on your phone, and you can show it in large text.

Phrases to show

이 주소로 가 주세요. 감사합니다!Please take me to this address. Thank you!
카드로 낼게요.I'll pay by card.
여기서 세워 주세요.Please stop here.
영수증 주세요.Receipt, please.
인천공항 제1터미널로 가 주세요.Incheon Airport, Terminal 1, please.
인천공항 제2터미널로 가 주세요.Incheon Airport, Terminal 2, please.
가장 가까운 지하철역으로 가 주세요.To the nearest subway station, please.

Good to know

  • Most taxis take credit cards; many also take transport cards (T-money). Have some cash just in case.
  • Fares go up late at night.
  • Incheon Airport has two terminals. Check which one your airline uses before you get in.
  • Taxi apps such as Kakao T are widely used in Korea.
