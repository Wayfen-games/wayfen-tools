How to Say “I’m Vegan” in Korean (and What to Ask About)

Many Korean dishes look vegetable-only but are made with animal-based stock or sauces. Saying “no meat” is often not enough. These phrases name the hidden ingredients so the staff knows exactly what to leave out.

The phrase to show

저는 비건이에요.I am vegan.
고기, 생선, 해산물, 달걀, 우유, 꿀을 먹지 못해요.I can't eat meat, fish, seafood, eggs, milk or honey.
멸치 육수, 사골 육수, 젓갈, 액젓, 어묵도 안 돼요.Anchovy stock, beef-bone stock, salted seafood, fish sauce and fish cake are also not OK.
이 재료를 빼고 만들 수 있는 음식이 있을까요?Is there a dish you can make without these?

Hidden ingredients to ask about

  • Kimchi usually contains fish sauce and salted shrimp.
  • Stews and soups such as doenjang-jjigae are often made with anchovy stock, even when no meat is visible.
  • Beef bone broth (사골) is the base of seolleongtang and gomtang.
  • Fish cake (어묵) is common in tteokbokki and gimbap.
  • Side dishes (반찬) can be seasoned with fish sauce.

Easier places to eat

Temple food (사찰음식) restaurants traditionally avoid meat and fish. When searching in Naver Map or KakaoMap, Korean keywords such as 비건 (vegan) or 채식 (vegetarian) usually work better than English.
