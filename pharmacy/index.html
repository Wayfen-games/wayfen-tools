Phrases for the ride

Pharmacy = 약국 (yak-guk). Look for a green or red 약 sign. Many close in the evening and on Sundays.

24-hour convenience stores sell a few basic medicines when pharmacies are closed: pain and fever relievers, cold medicine, digestive aids and pain patches. They do not sell medicine for diarrhea (as of Nov 2026).

Emergency: 119 (ambulance). Korea Travel Hotline: 1330 can help interpret (check current hours).

These cards help you describe how you feel. They are not medical advice. If it is serious, getting worse, or you are unsure, see a doctor or call 119.

Free to use. If this helped, please share it with other travelers.
