Hand-cut noodle soup made fresh every morning, 3 minutes from Anguk Station.

We make our noodles by hand every morning and simmer our broth with dried anchovy and kelp. Try our signature kalguksu (hand-cut noodle soup) or the mandu (Korean dumplings). English menu available. Card payment accepted.
